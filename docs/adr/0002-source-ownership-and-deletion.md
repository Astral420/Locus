# Source ownership governs retrieval and deletion

Documents belong independently to the Knowledge Base and may be linked to multiple meetings, so deleting a meeting removes links rather than the shared documents. Deletion excludes the source immediately, fences late job results, and cleans up derived content across storage; affected chat content is removed, including dependent turns that could retain deleted information. This requires provenance and recoverable cleanup rather than a single database delete, but preserves shared documents while honoring deletion of meeting content.
